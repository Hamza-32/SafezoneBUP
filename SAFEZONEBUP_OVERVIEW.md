# 🇧🇩 SafezoneBUP - Bangladesh University of Professionals Safety Platform

## 🎯 Project Overview

SafezoneBUP is a comprehensive campus safety platform specifically designed for **Bangladesh University of Professionals (BUP)**. The platform provides students, faculty, and staff with essential safety tools and resources tailored for the Bangladeshi educational environment.

## 🏛️ BUP-Specific Features

### 🇧🇩 **Localized Content**
- All contact information uses Bangladeshi phone numbers (+880 format)
- BUP email addresses (@bup.edu.bd)
- Campus locations specific to BUP Mirpur Campus
- Emergency services adapted for Bangladesh (999, 199, 10921)

## 🚀 **How to Use**

### For Students:
1. **Access Platform**: Visit SafezoneBUP portal
2. **Report Emergencies**: Use emergency button for urgent situations
3. **File Complaints**: Report facility or safety issues
4. **Lost & Found**: Post or search for lost items
5. **Discussion Forum**: Join anonymous support discussions
6. **Safety Check-In**: Use when traveling or in potentially unsafe situations

### For Faculty/Staff:
1. **Monitor Reports**: Review and respond to student reports
2. **Emergency Response**: Coordinate with campus security
3. **Resource Management**: Update safety resources and contacts
4. **Community Support**: Participate in safety discussions
